# gfot logo concept v1

Created 2026-10-20 with the built-in imagegen tool (not the CLI/API fallback). Original logo exploration inspired by the broad youthful geometric language of ByteDance-family brands.

Direction: electric cobalt and cyan, a recognizable single-storey lowercase g, a diagonal seam suggesting task continuity, and a navy lowercase gfot wordmark. Includes color, monochrome and app-tile studies.

This is a raster concept presentation, not a vector master or an exported production app icon. The 2026-10-21 implementation uses a vector reconstruction, shared by the README header and application UI/icon. See README.md in this directory for reproducible exports.

## Final refinement prompt

Refine this gfot brand presentation. Preserve the excellent cobalt/cyan palette, the bold dark navy 'gfot' wordmark, the landscape white composition and the three small bottom applications. Change ONLY the logo symbol in the main lockup and all three small applications consistently. The current symbol looks like an S or 3; replace it with an unmistakable, well-proportioned geometric SINGLE-STOREY LOWERCASE g monogram. Build the symbol from a nearly circular cobalt-blue upper bowl with a generous round counter, a right-hand stem and a descending cyan hook/tail curling left below the bowl. Keep the recognizable lowercase-g silhouette even at 24 pixels. ONE clever diagonal seam between the blue bowl and cyan descending hook may suggest forward motion/continuity, but it must not turn the letter into an S, double chevron or infinity glyph. Visually independent of the ordinary g in the wordmark: slightly squared outer curvature, bold substantial geometry, precise small diagonal cut, clean rounded terminals. Flat vector-like fills; smooth cyan/cobalt boundary; no textures, shadows, 3D or glow. The icon feels youthful, confident and precise, broadly in the design language of ByteDance-family products without copying a particular brand. Keep exact lowercase text 'gfot' unchanged; no other text. Bottom strip shows this SAME new g symbol in full color, in pure black, and in white on the blue rounded app tile.
